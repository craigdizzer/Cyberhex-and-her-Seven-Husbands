Cyberhex and Her 7 Husbands

Deep in the Whispering Pine Woods, Queen Cyberhex rules her gothic manor from a pile of velvet pillows, strictly allergic to physical labor and permanently locked into her mobile empire. Luckily, her seven fiercely devoted heavy-metal husbands handle everything from domestic chaos to supernatural crises so their queen never has to lift a finger.
